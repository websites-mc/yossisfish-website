What we carry

Raw or ready to bake — your choice

Every piece is handled by people who know fish. Tell us how you want it and we’ll take care of the rest.

Fresh Raw Fish

Salmon, tilapia, flounder, sea bass, cod, whitefish, carp and more, delivered fresh and kept on ice. Whole, filleted, or steaked.

Ready to Bake

Seasoned and marinated fillets packed in oven-ready trays. Take it home, slide it in the oven, and dinner is done.

Cleaned & Cut to Order

Skin on or off, bones out, portioned for your family or your simcha. Just tell us at the counter or when you call.
